I am a PhD student at Indiana University working with [Prof. Katy Börner](https://cns-iu.github.io/katy/). I study how AI agents can safely and effectively help people understand complex, large-scale data through visualization.

My work sits between human–computer interaction, visualization, and AI. I build systems and develop theory on how AI execution can work together with human capabilities, while staying grounded in human values. The questions I care about are practical ones: which parts of an analysis an agent should carry out, what a person needs to see to check that work, and how to keep the person in charge of what the data is taken to mean.

My current application areas are biomedical data and general-purpose data visualization. Outside research, I write essays on topics unrelated to my work, collected under [Writing](#writing).
