How can AI agents help people make sense of data that is too large or complex to explore alone, without taking judgment away from them? I approach this question by building agentic visualization systems and by developing theory about how AI execution and human capabilities should be combined.

#### Agentic visualization systems
Systems in which AI agents plan analyses, transform data, and produce visualizations, while people direct the questions and check the results.

#### Dividing work between agents and people
Theory on which analysis steps an agent should carry out and which depend on human expertise, judgment, and context.

#### Safe and human-centered AI assistance
Designs that keep agent actions visible, reviewable, and reversible, so that AI help reflects the goals and values of the people using it.

#### Biomedical and general data visualization
Applying these ideas to complex biomedical datasets and to general-purpose visualization tasks.
